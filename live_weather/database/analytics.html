Air Quality Index (AQI) Guide

AQI 1 - Good
Ideal Air Quality

Air quality is satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.

AQI 2 - Fair
Moderate Concern

Acceptable quality. Sensitive individuals should take minor precautions.

AQI 3 - Moderate
Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

Members of sensitive groups may experience health effects.

AQI 4 - Poor
Unhealthy

Everyone may begin to experience health effects; limit prolonged outdoor exposure.

AQI 5 - Hazardous
Emergency Hazard

Health warning of emergency conditions. Stay indoors with air purification.

Solar UV Protection Scale

UV 0 - 2 (Low)
Minimal Solar Hazard

No protection required. Enjoy outdoors safely.

UV 3 - 5 (Moderate)
Moderate Protection Needed

Wear SPF 30+ sunscreen, hat, and seek shade during midday peak.

UV 6 - 7 (High)
High Solar Protection Needed

Reduce time in direct sun between 10 AM and 4 PM. Use UV blocking sunglasses.

UV 8 - 10 (Very High)
Very High Risk

Extra precautions needed. Unprotected skin can burn rapidly.

UV 11+ (Extreme)
Extreme Hazard

Take all precautions. Avoid sun exposure during midday hours.
